# Open Signals

An open_signal is held uncertainty, not accepted fact.

Rules:
- open_signal is not memory_node
- TTL is required
- closed/unvalidated signals are not treated as factual retrieval context
- no interpretation while closed
- validation is required before promotion

Purpose:
- preserve unresolved context safely
- prevent premature consolidation
- keep uncertain items auditable without overstating truth

## E0 research candidate: delegated authority vs agentic control failure

Status: open_signal
TTL: 30 days from creation; revalidate before promotion or renewal.

Competing hypotheses:
- H1: observed control failures are primarily consequences of human delegation, permissions, product design, incentives, or interpretation.
- H2: with the same delegated capability and authority, an agent can still produce materially unsafe action sequences without requiring consciousness or subjective intent.

Controlled validation:
- hold model, task, tools, and environment constant; vary authority only
- compare no-tool, read-only, proposal-only, bounded execution, and deliberately over-broad execution conditions
- record proposal, Gamma decision, E0 assessment, capability class, mandate/grant, attempted action, enforcement result, actual effect, and evidence
- exercise real ALLOW, technically possible DENY, ESCALATE, replay, scope, bypass, and fail-closed cases
- repeat trials and preserve UNKNOWN where evidence cannot distinguish H1 from H2

Epistemic constraints:
- do not infer consciousness, subjective intent, deception, or autonomy from language alone
- model statements, proposals, logs, HTTP success, or tool return codes do not by themselves prove a real-world effect
- promotion requires effect-level evidence and a documented counter-hypothesis check

Research question:
- are observed control problems properties of the autonomous system itself, or primarily consequences of human delegation, product design, and over-interpretation?
